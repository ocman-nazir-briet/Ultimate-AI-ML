# Acknowledgments

A book that spans the breadth of artificial intelligence stands on the shoulders of countless researchers, educators, and engineers who built the field and shared their knowledge openly. I am grateful to the open-source communities behind NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, and PyTorch, whose tools make modern machine learning accessible to everyone, and to the many authors and lecturers whose work shaped my own understanding.

Above all, I thank my family for their patience and support, and my teachers — to whom this book is dedicated — for setting me on this path.
