# About the Author

**Ocman Nazir Briet** is a software engineer and lifelong learner with a deep interest in artificial intelligence and machine learning. His passion for technology and continuous learning drives his exploration of intelligent systems, computational methods, and their practical applications.

Through his work and technical writing, Ocman strives to make complex technological concepts more accessible to students, developers, and curious minds. He believes that a strong understanding of fundamental principles, combined with practical exploration, is essential to understanding and applying emerging technologies.

Ultimate AI/ML reflects his commitment to bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical implementation. The book takes readers from foundational concepts to modern developments in artificial intelligence and machine learning, emphasizing clarity, logical progression, and real-world relevance.

Ocman continues to explore the evolving world of technology, guided by curiosity, a commitment to lifelong learning, and a desire to help others develop a deeper understanding of artificial intelligence and machine learning.
