# Foreword

Few fields have moved as fast, or touched as many lives as quickly, as artificial intelligence. In the span of a single decade, machine learning has gone from a specialist’s tool to a technology that writes, sees, listens, and reasons alongside us. For anyone learning the field today, the challenge is no longer a scarcity of information but its overwhelming abundance, scattered across papers, blog posts, and documentation written for experts.

This book answers that challenge by gathering the essential ideas of artificial intelligence and machine learning into one coherent journey — from the mathematics that underpins a single neuron to the large language models and autonomous agents at the frontier. It does so without sacrificing rigor for accessibility, pairing every concept with intuition, illustration, and code. Whether you are taking your first steps or deepening a working knowledge, may it serve you well.
